SuprSend Analytics helps you understand what's working and what isn't in your notification system. Get real-time insights as trends update while messages go out, track performance across channels, and spot dips in engagement instantly.

**What you can do with Analytics:**

- **Real-time insights** → Track performance across channels and spot engagement dips instantly
- **Workflow-level comparisons** → Compare workflows, templates, channels, and categories side by side
- **Opt-out tracking** → See which channels/categories drive opt-outs before churn sets in
- **Over-messaging detection** → Track notifications per user and identify fatigue triggers by category
- **Granular filtering** → Multi-select filters for workflow, tenant, template, channel, category, and time range
- **Centralized error tracking** → All API, workflow, and delivery errors in one place—filter and debug in seconds

<Note>
  **Analytics vs Logs:**

  Analytics shows **aggregated insights** (trends, patterns, performance summaries). For detailed debugging of specific events, see [Logs](/docs/logging). Think of Analytics as the dashboard view, and Logs as the detailed inspection tool.
</Note>

## Analytics dashboard

When you open Analytics dashboard, you'll see three tabs:

## Performance Overview

Start here to get the big picture of your notification health. Get real-time insights as trends update while messages go out—track performance across channels and spot dips in engagement instantly. This tab shows you three key metrics:

1. **Channel performance** → Which channels are working best?
2. **Average notifications per user** → Are you over-messaging?
3. **Unsubscription trends** → Are users opting out?

### Channel performance

**Which channels are actually working?**

Compare delivery and engagement rates across channels to see where users are most responsive.

![](/images/channel-perf.png)

**How to read:**
- Click channel tabs (Email, SMS, Push, etc.) to switch views
- Summary numbers show delivery %, seen %, and click % for that channel
- Trend line shows how metrics change over time (date-wise)
- Warning icons indicate tracking issues—hover for details

**What to watch for:**

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| High delivery, low seen | Messages arriving but not opened | Improve subject lines, try different send times |
| High seen, low click | Users viewing but not engaging | Review content, strengthen CTAs |
| Declining trends | Performance dropping over time | Check for recent template, timing, or frequency changes |

### Average notifications per user

**Are you overwhelming your users?**

Track over-messaging trends: monitor average notifications per user, find patterns by category, and identify fatigue triggers to keep communications helpful—not noisy. Track notifications per user per day to identify fatigue patterns before users start ignoring you or unsubscribing.

![](/images/avg-notif.png)

**How to read:**
- Average number shows notifications per user per day
- Heatmap shows user distribution across volume ranges
- Darker areas = more users in that range

<Note>
  **Volume guidelines:**

  10+ notifications per day per user is often a fatigue risk for consumer apps. For high-frequency domains (trading, ride-hailing, operations), this may be normal. Filter by category first—marketing volume is usually the driver. Evaluate by category and check opt-out correlation.
</Note>

**When you see high volume:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by category">
    Find which notification types contribute most to volume
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Workflow Level Breakdown">
    Identify workflows sending frequently
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consider batching or digest">
    Group non-urgent notifications together
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cross-check with opt-out trends">
    See if high-volume users are unsubscribing
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Unsubscription trends

**Why are users leaving?**

Know when your users opt-out. See which channels and categories drive opt-outs so you can adjust before churn sets in. Monitor opt-out rates by channel and category to understand what's driving users away.

![](/images/users-optout.png)

**Benchmarks (use as reference):**

| Opt-out Rate | What it means |
|--------------|---------------|
| Below 0.2% | ✅ Excellent |
| 0.2-0.5% | ⚠️ Acceptable |
| Above 0.5% | 🔴 Needs attention |

**What to investigate:**
- **Spike in opt-outs** → Check if it correlates with average notifications per user
- **Category-specific** → Marketing showing high opt-outs? Consider reducing frequency
- **Channel-specific** → Some channels may not fit your audience

**Next steps:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cross-reference with average notifications">
    Check for over-messaging correlation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by category">
    Identify problematic notification types
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review templates and timing">
    For high opt-out categories
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consider preference centers">
    Give users control over frequency
  </Step>
  </Steps>

## Workflow Level Breakdown

Use workflow-level comparisons to compare workflows, templates, channels, and categories side by side to spot underperformers and validate experiments. When you spot issues in Performance Overview, use this tab to drill down and identify exactly which workflows, templates, or vendors are causing problems.

![](/images/wf-level.png)

**What you can discover:**
- Channel performance by workflow
- Template-level insights
- Vendor performance comparison
- Category analysis (transactional vs marketing)

## Errors

**What's breaking and how do I fix it?**

Centralized error tracking: all API, workflow, and provider delivery errors in one place. Filter by tenant/workflow/template/channel, open the exact log, and debug in seconds. When delivery drops or notifications fail, use this tab to identify and fix issues quickly. For step-by-step guides to resolve specific error types, see [Error guides](/docs/error-guides).

### Triage checklist

**Delivery dropped? Start here:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check API Request Failures">
    Are API failures spiking? Requests aren't reaching SuprSend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Workflow Execution Failures">
    Are workflow failures spiking? Workflows failing after API calls succeed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Delivery Failures">
    Are delivery failures spiking by vendor or channel? Vendors rejecting or failing to deliver.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### API Request Failures

**What this shows:** Errors from API or SDK calls that failed before reaching SuprSend.

**When to check:** Low delivery rates or missing notifications.

| Error Type | Common Causes |
|------------|---------------|
| Authentication errors | Expired credentials, wrong workspace |
| Payload errors | Missing required fields, invalid formats |
| Rate limiting | Too many requests in short time |

**How to fix:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click on error">
    View detailed error message and request payload
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check integration code">
    Look for the specific error type
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review error guides">
    See [error guides](/docs/error-guides) for resolution
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify API keys">
    Ensure they're correct and not expired
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Workflow / Broadcast Execution Failures

**What this shows:** Errors during workflow execution that caused nodes to fail.

**When to check:** Notifications aren't being sent even though API calls succeed.

**Common issues:**
- Template rendering errors (missing variables, syntax problems)
- Node failures (fetch, transform, etc.)
- Data validation errors

**How to fix:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click on error">
    See which workflow and node failed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review workflow configuration">
    Check settings for the failing node
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check data payload">
    Ensure required fields are present and formatted correctly
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review error guides">
    See [error guides](/docs/error-guides) for workflow-specific resolution steps
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test workflow">
    Use sample data to reproduce
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Notification Delivery Failures

**What this shows:** Errors reported by vendors when they fail to deliver notifications.

**When to check:** Low delivery rates but API calls and workflows succeed.

**Common issues:**
- Vendor-specific errors (invalid credentials, rate limits, outages)
- Channel-specific problems (invalid email addresses, phone numbers, push tokens)
- Template mismatches (WhatsApp template approval, email formatting)

**How to fix:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click on error">
    View vendor error message and details
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check vendor status page">
    See if vendor is experiencing an outage
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify vendor credentials">
    Ensure vendor configuration is correct
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review error guides">
    Check [error guides](/docs/error-guides) for vendor-specific steps
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jump to Logs">
    For detailed debugging of specific events, use [Logs](/docs/logging). **Switch to Logs when:** You need to investigate a specific failed notification or see the exact error message for a particular event.
  </Step>
  </Steps>

### Common scenarios

<Accordion title="Scenario 1: Delivery suddenly dropped">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check API Request Failures">

    </Step>
    <Step title="Check Delivery Failures">

    </Step>
    <Step title="Review recent changes">
      Check if integration code or API keys were recently updated
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify API keys">
      Ensure credentials are correct and not expired

<Accordion title="Scenario 3: Workflow failures for specific workflow">

    </Step>
    <Step title="Check data payload">

## Best practices

<Accordion title="Regular monitoring schedule">
  - **Weekly reviews** → Check Performance Overview to catch trends early
  - **Monthly deep dives** → Use Workflow Level Breakdown to find optimization opportunities
  - **Error monitoring** → Check Errors tab daily during setup, then weekly once stable
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Effective analysis approach">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Start broad, then narrow">
      Begin with 30-day Performance Overview, then drill down
    </Step>
    <Step title="Compare similar workflows">
      Identify what's working in high performers and apply to underperformers
    </Step>
    <Step title="Correlate metrics">
      When opt-outs spike, check average notifications. When engagement drops, check errors.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Track changes">
      Document template/workflow changes and monitor impact
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Optimization workflow">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Identify problem">
      Use Performance Overview to spot low engagement or high opt-outs
    </Step>
    <Step title="Drill down">
      Use Workflow Level Breakdown to find specific workflows
    </Step>
    <Step title="Investigate">
      Check Errors tab, review templates and send times
    </Step>
    <Step title="Test changes">
      Make improvements and monitor impact
    </Step>
    <Step title="Iterate">
      Keep optimizing based on data
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Automation and alerts">
  - Set up [outbound webhooks](/docs/outbound-webhook) to alert when error rates exceed thresholds
  - Monitor opt-out rates and average notifications per user to catch issues early
  - Export data via [Amazon S3 sync](/docs/amazon_s3_v2) for automated reporting and dashboards
</Accordion>

## FAQs

<Accordion title="Why don't I see seen or clicked metrics?">
  You need to enable tracking first. Configure the SuprSend tracking URL `https://hub.suprsend.com/webhook/*` in your vendor portal. This enables SuprSend to get delivery and engagement responses from your vendor. You'll find details of the callback URL in individual [vendor integration documentation](/docs/vendors).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How fresh is the analytics data?">
  Analytics data is updated in near real-time. There may be a short delay (typically a few minutes) for recent events to appear in the dashboard. For immediate verification of specific events, use [Logs](/docs/logging).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference between Analytics and Logs?">
  **Analytics** shows aggregated insights—trends, patterns, and performance summaries across all your notifications. **Logs** shows detailed, event-level information for individual notifications. Use Analytics to spot patterns, then switch to Logs when you need to investigate specific events in detail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I know if I'm sending too many notifications?">
  Check the "Average notifications per user" metric in Performance Overview. 10+ notifications per user per day is often a fatigue risk for consumer apps. Filter by category first—marketing volume is usually the driver. Also cross-reference with opt-out trends to see if high-volume users are unsubscribing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My delivery rate is low—where do I start?">
  Start with the Errors tab triage checklist: Check API Request Failures first, then Workflow Execution Failures, then Delivery Failures. Filter by vendor or channel to spot patterns. For detailed debugging of specific events, use [Logs](/docs/logging).
</Accordion>
