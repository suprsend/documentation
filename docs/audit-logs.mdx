Audit logs provide a complete record of all actions performed by team members on your SuprSend account. Each entry includes who performed the action, when it happened, and details about what changed.

This is particularly useful for maintaining team accountability, tracking security-sensitive operations, and quickly identifying who made specific changes—especially helpful when someone accidentally deletes a service token or makes an unintended configuration change.

<Note>
  Audit logs are available on the Enterprise plan.
</Note>

## Accessing audit logs

To view all account activity:

1. Go to **Account Settings** in the SuprSend dashboard
2. Click on the [Audit Logs tab](https://app.suprsend.com/en/account-settings/audit-logs)

The audit logs page displays a list showing the actor, action, workspace, location, and date for each entry.

![](/images/audit-logs.png)

Filter and search audit logs to find specific entries:

- **Search**: Find particular actions or events
- **Filter by date range**: Choose relative date ranges (e.g., last 7 days, last 30 days) or set an absolute date range
- **Filter by actor**: View actions performed by specific team members
- **Filter by action**: Narrow down to specific action types (e.g., `service_token.deleted`, `vendor.added`)

<Note>
  **📘 Understanding audit log entries:**
  
  Each audit log entry shows who performed the action, when it happened, and relevant details. Action descriptions include dynamic values (like email addresses, key names, or endpoint URLs) that are automatically filled in when the event is logged.
</Note>

## Tracked actions

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I export my audit logs?" defaultOpen={false}>
    Please contact the SuprSend support team if you need to export your audit logs. They'll help you export the data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How far back do audit logs go?" defaultOpen={false}>
    Audit log retention depends on your pricing plan. Check the [pricing page](https://www.suprsend.com/pricing) to see the log retention timeframe for your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I see system actions in my audit logs?" defaultOpen={false}>
    When events occur that don't originate from a user action (like automated system updates or side effects from merges), these events may be attributed to system processes. This helps maintain a complete audit trail of all changes to your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I filter audit logs?" defaultOpen={false}>
    You can filter audit logs using:
    - **Search**: Find specific actions or events
    - **Date range**: Filter by time period (last 7 days, last 30 days, or custom range)
    - **Actor**: See actions performed by specific team members
    - **Action type**: Filter by specific action types like `service_token.deleted` or `vendor.added`
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
