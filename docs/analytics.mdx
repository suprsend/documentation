See how your notifications are performing and identify what's working—and what needs fixing—with SuprSend Analytics. Navigate to the [Analytics tab](https://app.suprsend.com/en/production/analytics/) to access your analytics.

<Note>
  **Analytics vs Logs:**

  **Analytics** shows you the big picture—trends, patterns, and summaries across all your notifications. [**Logs**](/docs/logging) shows you individual events in detail. Think of Analytics as your dashboard showing overall health, and Logs as your detailed inspection tool for specific issues.
</Note>

Analytics provides three main views to help you understand and optimize your notification performance:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Performance Overview" icon="chart-line" iconType="solid" href="#performance-overview">
    See how all your notifications are performing overall
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workflow Level Breakdown" icon="layer-group" iconType="solid" href="#workflow-level-breakdown">
    Compare specific workflows and find what needs improvement
  </Card>
  <Card title="Errors" icon="exclamation" iconType="solid" href="#errors">
    Find and fix problems quickly
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **How to use these views together:**

  Use [**Performance Overview**](#performance-overview) to *spot* issues (low engagement, high opt-outs). Use [**Workflow Level Breakdown**](#workflow-level-breakdown) to *locate* which workflows are causing problems. Use [**Errors**](#errors) and [**Logs**](/docs/logging) to *debug* specific failures.
</Note>

## Analytics Views

### Performance Overview

**Performance Overview shows you the big picture of your notification system's health.** This tab highlights three key signals that help you understand overall performance and user engagement across all your notifications.

<Frame caption="Performance Overview dashboard">
  ![](/images/channel-perf.png)
</Frame>

**What you can see:**

- **Channel performance** — Compare delivery, seen, and click rates across all channels (email, SMS, push, etc.)
- **Average notifications per user per day** — Track how many notifications each user receives to avoid over-messaging
- **Unsubscription trends** — Monitor opt-out rates by channel and category to understand what's driving users away

**What to watch for:**

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| High delivery, low seen | Messages are arriving but people aren't opening them | Improve subject lines, try different send times, or review content |
| High seen, low click | People are viewing but not clicking | Improve your content or make your call-to-action clearer |
| 10+ notifications per user per day | You might be sending too many notifications | Filter by category to find the culprit, then reduce frequency or batch notifications |
| Opt-out rate above 0.5% | Too many people are unsubscribing | Check if it correlates with notification volume, investigate specific categories or channels |

**Navigate from here:**
- **Channel underperforming?** → Go to [**Workflow Level Breakdown**](#workflow-level-breakdown) and filter by that channel
- **High notification volume?** → Filter by category, then check [**Workflow Level Breakdown**](#workflow-level-breakdown) to find specific workflows
- **Spike in opt-outs?** → Cross-reference with "Average notifications per user" metric—over-messaging is often the cause

### Workflow Level Breakdown

**Workflow Level Breakdown lets you compare performance across workflows, templates, channels, vendors, and categories side by side.** Use this table view to identify which notifications are working and which aren't.

<Frame caption="Workflow Level Breakdown view">
  ![](/images/wf-level.png)
</Frame>

The table shows one row per unique workflow + delivery path combination with columns for workflow, template, channel, vendor, category, count, delivery rate, seen rate, and click rate.

**How to use it:**

1. **Apply filters** — Use the filters panel to narrow down by workflow, template, channel, vendor, category, or tenant
2. **Sort columns** — Click on any column header to sort by that metric (e.g., sort by seen rate to find underperformers)
3. **Compare rows** — Compare similar workflows side by side to identify what's working and what needs improvement

**What to watch for:**

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| One workflow has much lower engagement than others | That workflow needs attention | Investigate the [template](/docs/templates), timing, or content. Compare with high-performing workflows |
| One vendor has lower delivery rates than others | That vendor might be having issues | Check vendor status page, verify [vendor configuration](/docs/vendors), or consider switching vendors |

**Navigate from here:**
- **One channel underperforming?** → Filter by that channel to see which workflows are dragging it down
- **Want to find best practices?** → Sort by seen/click rate to see top performers
- **Need detailed debugging?** → Go to [**Errors**](#errors) tab or [**Logs**](/docs/logging) for event-level details

### Errors

**Errors tab helps you find and fix problems quickly.** All errors are organized in one place, making it easy to identify and resolve issues affecting your notifications.

<Frame caption="Errors view">
  ![](/images/error.png)
</Frame>

**What you'll see:**

- **API Request Failures** — Your code isn't reaching SuprSend (authentication issues, invalid requests, etc.)
- **Workflow Execution Failures** — Workflows are failing after they start (template errors, missing data, etc.)
- **Notification Delivery Failures** — Vendors can't deliver notifications (invalid email addresses, vendor outages, etc.)

**What to watch for:**

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Sudden spike in API Request Failures | Your integration might have an issue | Check API keys, verify request format, review recent code changes |
| High Workflow Execution Failures | Workflows are failing during execution | Check [templates](/docs/templates) for errors, verify data payloads, review [workflow configuration](/docs/workflow-builder) |
| Delivery Failures from one vendor | That vendor might be having problems | Check vendor status page, verify [vendor configuration](/docs/vendors), consider switching vendors |

**Example:**

A sudden spike in **Workflow Execution Failures** often happens after a template change where a required variable is missing. Filter by workflow to identify the affected workflow, then check the [template](/docs/templates) for missing variables.

**Navigate from here:**
- **Need detailed error information?** → Click on any error to see the exact message and context
- **Want step-by-step debugging?** → Go to [**Logs**](/docs/logging) to see individual event details and execution traces
- **Looking for solutions?** → Check [error guides](/docs/error-guides) for vendor-specific and error-type-specific resolution steps

## Understanding the Metrics

### Metrics overview

| Metric | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Delivered** | Notification successfully sent to user's device |
| **Seen** | Notification viewed or opened by the user. For Android push channel, this also includes the notifications cleared from the tray |
| **Clicked** | User clicked on the notification |
| **Opt-out** | The user unsubscribed from notifications |

## Navigating Analytics Page

### Refresh Interval

Analytics data updates automatically, but you can click the refresh button to update it manually. Data refreshes as vendors send status updates to SuprSend.

### Filters

**Global filters** (date range, timezone) apply everywhere across all Analytics tabs.

**Tab-specific filters** (workflow, tenant, template, channel, category, vendor, notification status) only apply to the current view.

**Fastest ways to find something:**
- **Looking for a specific workflow's performance?** → Filter by workflow name in [**Workflow Level Breakdown**](#workflow-level-breakdown)
- **Checking one channel?** → Use the channel filter or click channel tabs in [**Performance Overview**](#performance-overview)
- **Investigating errors?** → Go to [**Errors**](#errors) tab and filter by error type or vendor
- **Comparing workflows?** → Use [**Workflow Level Breakdown**](#workflow-level-breakdown) with multiple filters

### Time range

Choose from relative ranges (Last 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, and additional presets) or select absolute date ranges with timezone-aware selection.

**Absolute range:** Selecting in your timezone (e.g., Asia/Kolkata). You can view data for max 7 days at a time.

Recent data (last 24 hours) may still be updating as vendors send status updates. After 48 hours, data is considered final.

### Data Retention

Analytics default to "Last 7 days" when first opened. Older data is retained for a period of time based on your [pricing plan](https://www.suprsend.com/pricing).

For extended retention options, contact our [sales team](mailto:sales@suprsend.com).

## Exporting Analytics

Export your analytics data as CSV files directly from any Analytics tab.

<Frame caption="Export analytics dialog">
  ![](/images/export-analytics.png)
</Frame>

**Export types:**
- **Aggregate user data** — Totals grouped by date and time, matching what you see in the charts
- **Raw user data** — One row per user with timestamp of each action

**Date range:** Choose relative range (e.g., "Last 7 days") or absolute range (specific date range).

**Filters:** Apply filters for channel, template, notification status, workflow, category, tenant, or vendor before exporting. The CSV will only include data matching your current filters and date range.

**How to export:**
1. Navigate to the Analytics tab you want to export
2. Click the three dots (⋯) in the top right of that section
3. Click on "Export as CSV"
4. Select your date range (relative or absolute)
5. Choose your export type (aggregate or raw user data)
6. Apply any filters you want to include
7. Download the CSV file

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Why don't I see 'seen' or 'clicked' metrics?">
    You need to enable tracking first. Set up the SuprSend tracking URL `https://hub.suprsend.com/webhook/*` in your vendor account. This lets SuprSend receive delivery and engagement updates from your vendor. See your [vendor integration docs](/docs/vendors) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How up-to-date is the data?">
    Data updates in near real-time, usually within a few minutes. For the most recent hour, data might still be updating (expect some variance). For events from 1-24 hours ago, most data is in. After 48 hours, data is final and won't change. For immediate verification of a specific event, use [Logs](/docs/logging).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Analytics and Logs?">
    **Analytics** shows you trends and summaries across all your notifications (like "email has 45% open rate"). [**Logs**](/docs/logging) shows you individual events in detail (like "this specific email to user@example.com was delivered at 2:34 PM"). Use Analytics to spot patterns, then use [**Logs**](/docs/logging) to investigate specific events.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know if I'm sending too many notifications?">
    Check the "Average notifications per user" metric in [**Performance Overview**](#performance-overview). If it's 10+ per day, that's often too much for most apps (users might get annoyed). Filter by category first—marketing notifications are usually the main driver. Also check if users receiving lots of notifications are unsubscribing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My delivery rate is low—where do I start?">
    Start with the [**Errors**](#errors) tab. Check API Request Failures first (are your requests even reaching SuprSend?), then Workflow Execution Failures (are workflows failing?), then Delivery Failures (are vendors rejecting notifications?). Filter by vendor or channel to spot patterns. For detailed debugging of specific events, use [**Logs**](/docs/logging).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do my numbers keep changing?">
    Analytics update as vendors send us status updates. If a vendor is slow to report (common with international SMS), the numbers will update when we receive the update. Data from the last 48 hours might change slightly. After 48 hours, data is final.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my analytics data?">
    Yes. You can export analytics data as CSV files directly from any Analytics tab. Choose between aggregate user data (totals grouped by date/time) or raw user data (one row per user with timestamps).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What timezone is the data shown in?">
    Analytics use your browser's timezone by default. You can change it in the top navbar if needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long is data kept?">
    Data retention depends on your plan. Check the [pricing page](https://www.suprsend.com/pricing) for details.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
