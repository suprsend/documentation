## Pre-Requisites

You'll need a Bird account to get started. Integrate with SuprSend using your existing account, or [get started with new Bird account](https://app.bird.com/onboarding/tell-about-yourself)

## Bird Integration on SuprSend Platform

On the SuprSend dashboard, navigate to the Vendors page from the side panel. **Select SMS -> Type (System/ Transactional/ Promotional) -> Bird** from the list of available vendors. This will open the vendor details page as shown below:

![](/images/docs/bird-vendor-on-suprsend-ui.png)

| Form Field                      | Description                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nickname                        | A custom identifier name that helps you easily distinguish this account easily                                                                              |
| Workspace ID                    | Access your workspace key from the SuprSend Dashboard by selecting `Developers` -> `API Keys` from left side-panel.                                         |
| AccessKey                       | This is the same `Access Key` that you will be creating on the Bird dashboard. (Admin Console -> Settings -> Security -> Access Key)                        |
| API Type                        | API Type could either be `Channel` or `Navigator` to be selected. Know more about each in detail [here](/docs/bird-sms#types-of-access-keys-in-bird)        |
| Channel ID / Navigator ID       | Unique Identifier for the channel /navigator selected for sending sms notifications.                                                                        |
| Price per notifiscation          | This is the amount you pay per SMS notification to Bird. It helps us to calculate, estimate and optimise your cost spent on notifications.                  |

### How to get Workspace ID from Bird Platform?

Get your `workspace-id` by doing to Bird's Dashboard, and following the flow as:
 1. Go to **"Admin Console"** from the bootom-left corner of the page.
 2. From the list, click on **Settings -> Workspaces**. This will open a page, where you can find all the workspaces, and their `workspace-id`, in your account.
 3. Click on the desired worksapce, and upon this you will find it's `workspace-id`.

 ![](/images/docs/workspace-id-bird.png)

### How to get Access Keys from Bird Dashboard?

In order to get the Access Keys from the Bird dashboard, firstly Login to your Bird account.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Go to the 'Admin Console' on the Bird Dashboard">
    From the bottom-right section, navigate to the `Admin Console` section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on the 'Settings' -> 'Security' option further from the side-bar">
    Find the **"Access Key"** section under the Settings -> Security section of the left panel list.
    You will land a page which displays the `Add new Access Key` button.
    ![Access Key Dashboardf for Bird](/images/docs/bird-doc-api-key.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate New Access Key">
    After clicking the `Add new Access Key` button, a new page pops-up and displays the **Access Key** section.

    **Copy the generated Key / Token**. You will need to enter this on SuprSend platform for integration.


    Click on **Save**. Your key will be saved on the dashboard!

    ![Access Key Generated Message](/images/docs/access-key-bird-pop.png)

    <Warning>
        Once created, the Access Key will only be displayed once. So, make sure to save it for future references, or simply copy-paste it in the SuprSend dashboard,
    </Warning>

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Types of Access Keys in Bird

There are two types of Access Keys in Bird:

![](/images/docs/api-type-bird.png)

1. **Channel**: Each phone number purchased on Bird platform needs to be added to a channel mandatorily. Hence, each phone number will also have a unique channel ID assigned to it. If you'd like to send SMS via a specific phone number, it could be done by using its unique `Channel ID`.
2. **Navigator**: It is essentially a group of channels, and is identified by a unique `Navigator ID` for each navigator. This API type can be helpful in sending SMS notifications by users who use multiple phone numbers, by which it can select the suitable channel, (i.e. the phone number associated) to be used for any given SMS notification. This selection is made on the basis of region/country of the phone numbers.

## How to purchase a Bird phone number?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Selecting Category on the sidebar">
    In the left sidepanel, click on the `Developer` category.
    ![](/images/docs/buy-bird-number-step-1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Selecting 'Buy a Number' option">
    Now, from the options available, click on `Numbers` dropdown. Further, click on `Phone numbers` from the list.
    ![](/images/docs/buy-bird-number-step-2.png)
      <Tip>
        Instead of these steps, A direct URL can be visited, witht he help of your `workspace-id` :
        <CodeBlock>
          {`https://app.bird.com/workspaces/<your-workspace-id>/channels/numbers/list`}
        </CodeBlock>
      </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You will land to the 'Phone Numbers' page">
    This is the desired page where you will be able to purchase a phone number on Bird Platform.
    ![](/images/docs/buy-bird-number-page.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click on the 'Buy a Number' button">
    You will land on a page where you can search for any desirable number according to your choice of country.
    ![](/images/docs/buy-number-screen-bird.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add desirable number to the cart">
    Select the phone number you want to buy, and click on the 'Add to Cart' button. This is how the cart would look after you've opted for the phone number of your choice.
    ![](/images/docs/sample-number-bird.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final Payment Step">
    On making suitable payments according to your cart price, you will be able to access the number on your Bird dashboard.
    <Warning>
      After adding a number to cart, you will have 30 minutes to reserve and buy the number. If you upon delaying payments, the number would be removed from the cart.
    </Warning>
    ![](/images/docs/add-to-cart-bird.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Installing Phone Number to a Channel

After successfully purchasing phone number on Bird, you will need to **install this number to a channel**.

You can do this by clicking on **"Install to channel"** button. After doing so, a new page will open, displaying the `channel name`, `channel use case`, `status`, and `type of message`.

Fill the field as per your requirements, and click on **"Install SMS by Bird"** button.

  ![Install phone number to channel page](/images/docs/number-to-channel.png)


### How to get Channel/ Navigator ID on Bird?

**(1) For Channel-ID:**
    - Go to **"Developer"** from the left sidepanel.
    - From the list, click on **"SMS"** under the Channels group. Further, click on **"SMS Setup"**.
    - You will see a list of channels, with their respective `ChannelID`.
    ![](/images/docs/get-channel-id-bird.png)

**(2) For Navigator-ID:**
    - Go to **"Developer"** from the left sidepanel.
    - From the list, click on **"Navigators"** under the Products group. Further, click on **"Navigators Setup"**.
    - You will see a list of channels, with their respective `NavigatorID`.
    ![](/images/docs/get-navigator-id-bird.png)


## Managing Webhook URLs

One of the platform advantage of using SuprSend as a central communication system is that it shows notification analytics for all channels in your SuprSend account together.

**Creating Webhook URLs on Bird UI:**

Upon configuring the SuprSend Webhook URL in Bird, you can get track the message delivery status and failure logs on SuprSend dashboard. If you have a requirement where you would want to get the DLR reports in your backend, contact us at [support@suprsend.com](mailto:support@suprsend.com)

![Creating Webhook URL on Bird UI](/images/docs/webhook-url-on-bird.png)

**Creating Webhook URLs on SuprSend UI:**

Whereas, If you're configuring the Bird Webhook URL on SuprSend, track the message delivery status from SuprSend on your Bird dashboard. This could be a great help in case of **outbound-notications**.
