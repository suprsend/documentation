MS Teams is a chat channel — you can deliver to a Teams channel via incoming webhook or via a Teams bot for richer interactions. The setup has more pieces than Email/SMS because you need to publish a Teams App.

Setup is three steps:

1. **Create a Teams App** (bot or webhook).
2. **Enable the MS Teams vendor** in SuprSend and link your App.
3. **Add an MS Teams delivery node** with a Teams template.

---

## 1. Create a Teams App

Follow the [Microsoft Teams vendor guide](/docs/microsoft-teams):
- [Step 1 — Create Teams App](/docs/microsoft-teams#step-1-create-teams-app)
- [Step 2 — Add Teams integration on SuprSend](/docs/microsoft-teams#step-2-add-teams-integration-on-suprsend)

For a webhook-only integration (simpler, no bot), users add an Incoming Webhook to a Teams channel and you store that URL as the recipient identity.

---

## 2. Enable the MS Teams vendor

Add your Teams App credentials on the SuprSend vendor page. For multi-tenant B2B, configure [per-tenant Teams vendors](/docs/tenant-vendor) so each customer connects their own Teams instance.

---

## Recipient identity field

The MS Teams identity is `$ms_teams`. Format depends on whether you're using a bot or a webhook:

```json
{
  "distinct_id": "user_123",
  "$ms_teams": {
    "tenant_id": "...",
    "service_url": "https://smba.trafficmanager.net/...",
    "conversation_id": "..."
  }
}
```

For webhook delivery, the identity is the webhook URL.

---

## 3. MS Teams template

Use the [MS Teams template editor](/docs/ms-teams-template). For simple text, use the Markdown editor with `{{var}}` Handlebars. For rich Adaptive Cards, switch to the [JSONNET editor](/docs/jsonnet-templates) — variables become `data.var` (or `data["$batched_events"][0].var` for batched alerts). **Publish** before saving the workflow.

---

## MS Teams gotchas

- **Adaptive Cards** are the way to do anything richer than plain text. Use the [Adaptive Card Designer](https://adaptivecards.io/designer/) to prototype, then paste the JSON into the JSONNET template editor.
- **Per-tenant in B2B:** every customer org should install your Teams App in their own tenant. Use [per-tenant vendors](/docs/tenant-vendor).
- **App approval:** publishing a Teams App in an organization may require admin approval depending on the customer's Microsoft 365 settings.
- **Rate limits:** Microsoft enforces per-tenant rate limits. Use the [Throttle](/docs/throttle) workflow setting for bulk sends.
