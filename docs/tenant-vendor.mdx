---
title: "Tenant Vendor"
description: "Set up per-tenant vendors in SuprSend to route notifications through your customer's own email, SMS, or push providers instead of the default company vendor."
---

When sending notifications for your enterprise customers, not just notification styling; your clients might also want the notifications to come from their own tenant handles. **You can use tenant vendor to route notifications through your customer's channel providers** instead of the default company vendor. If no tenant vendor is set, vendor config on the default tenant is used.

## How to add tenant vendors

You can add tenant vendors from the ["Vendor" page](https://app.suprsend.com/en/staging/vendors/) on the SuprSend dashboard. Select the desired tenant from the top right side and add vendors for relevant channels.
