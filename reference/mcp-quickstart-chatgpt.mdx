## Set up the connection

<Tabs>

<Tab title="ChatGPT">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the custom MCP flow">
    In ChatGPT, open **Plugins** in the sidebar, then click **Add** → **Add custom MCP server**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mcp-chatgpt-add-custom-server.png" alt="ChatGPT Plugins page with the Add menu open and Add custom MCP server highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the SuprSend URL">
    ```
    https://mcp.suprsend.com/mcp
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept the risk warning">
    ChatGPT warns that custom MCP servers can run tool calls on your account. Click **I understand and want to continue**, then **Create as a plugin**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with your SuprSend account">
    ChatGPT opens a sign-in popup. Sign in with your SuprSend account. Your role decides the tools: admins get every tool, and members get read-only tools.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    In a new chat, ask:

    ```
    List my SuprSend workspaces.
    ```

    ChatGPT answers with your SuprSend workspaces.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Codex CLI">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the SuprSend MCP server">
    ```bash
    codex mcp add suprsend --url https://mcp.suprsend.com/mcp
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with your SuprSend account">
    When you add the server, Codex opens your browser. Sign in with your SuprSend account. Your role decides the tools: admins get every tool, and members get read-only tools.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    In a Codex session, ask:

    ```
    List my SuprSend workspaces.
    ```

    Codex answers with your SuprSend workspaces.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## Pin a workspace and restrict tools

By default, ChatGPT and Codex can use every workspace and tool your role allows. To keep them on one workspace, or to give them only the tools a job needs, use one of these URLs when you add the server:

- **Pin a workspace** by adding its slug to the path. The agent then can't switch to another workspace:

  ```
  https://mcp.suprsend.com/mcp/staging
  ```

- **Restrict tools** with `?tools=`, a comma-separated list of tool names or groups:

  ```
  https://mcp.suprsend.com/mcp/staging?tools=users_*,data_*
  ```

See [Scope the connection](/reference/mcp-overview#scope-the-connection) for every URL pattern, and the [tool list](/reference/mcp-tool-list) for every tool.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Server shows disconnected or refuses the connection">
    Check the workspace slug in the URL. It must match a workspace in your SuprSend account exactly, for example `staging`, not the workspace's display name. A slug that doesn't exist fails with `403 workspace_forbidden`. To see your slugs, remove the slug, reconnect, and ask: "List my SuprSend workspaces."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
