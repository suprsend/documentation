Slack Agent brings the same agent you use in the dashboard ([In-product Agent](/reference/agent)) into your Slack workspace. Look up profiles, trigger workflows, change preferences, pull delivery analytics, and ask product questions, all from a DM or channel.

Two things to know before you install:

- **Privacy Filter is always on outside 1:1 DMs.** Replies are checked for PII before they're posted to a channel or group DM. A reply flagged as containing PII is shown only to the requester, as a private message in the thread; the channel sees a short notice. The check is model-based and best-effort: if it can't run, the reply is posted to the channel as usual. See [Privacy Filter](#privacy-filter) below.
- **AI is optional.** SuprSend's notification stack (workflows, templates, channels, preferences, delivery, analytics) does not depend on AI. If your organization can't share data with third-party model providers, don't connect the Slack App, and have an admin turn off the In-product Agent. See [Settings](#settings).

## Quick start

1. **Connect Slack.** A SuprSend admin clicks **Connect to Slack** in **Account Settings → [AI & Agents](https://app.suprsend.com/en/account-settings/ai-agents)** and approves the app in Slack.
2. After install, **`@SuprSend`** appears as a regular bot user in your workspace. DM it or invite it to any channel. Your Slack email must match a user in your SuprSend organization, or the bot can't sign you in.
3. Start asking. Name the SuprSend workspace in your request, for example staging or production. If you don't, Slack Agent asks which one to use:

   - In a channel: type **`@suprsend`** followed by your request.
   - In a DM: open a direct message with **`@SuprSend`** and just type.

```text
@suprsend show the profile for user_123

@suprsend trigger order-confirmation for jane@acme.com

@suprsend what was our email open rate last week?
```

## Privacy Filter

Slack channels are searchable, exportable, and often shared with broader audiences than the people who originally asked. A teammate posting *"what's jane@acme.com's phone number?"* into `#growth` shouldn't leak that number to everyone with channel history access.

The **Privacy Filter** is always on in channels and group DMs. It detects when a response would contain PII (emails, phone numbers, push tokens, device identifiers, channel addresses) and, if it does, keeps the reply out of the channel:

- The **channel** sees a short notice in-thread: *"This response contains sensitive information — I've sent it to you privately."*
- The **requester** gets the full reply as an ephemeral message in the same thread, visible only to them, so the person doing the work still gets what they need.

1:1 DMs are private, so the filter doesn't apply there, and full identifiers are returned inline. Group DMs are filtered like channels.

| Surface | When to use it | What the Privacy Filter does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1:1 direct messages** | Private lookups, work tied to a specific user, anything involving identifiers, channel addresses, or device tokens. | Inactive. Emails, phone numbers, push tokens, and other PII are returned in full. |
| **Channels and group DMs** | Team-visible work, like analytics summaries, workflow audits, debugging in front of teammates. | **Always active.** A reply with PII is shown only to the requester, as an ephemeral message; the channel sees a notice instead. |

The filter runs at the response layer, not the prompt layer. Teammates can still ask sensitive questions in channels; the filter checks the answer so it doesn't expose PII in a place it shouldn't live. Approval prompts for changes (see [Approvals](#approvals)) are posted as they are, without the filter.

For organizations that don't want any prompt or response data leaving SuprSend, don't connect the Slack App, and turn off the In-product Agent in [AI & Agents](https://app.suprsend.com/en/account-settings/ai-agents). See [Settings](#settings) below.

## Approvals

Before Slack Agent changes users, objects, tenants, lists or preferences, tracks an event or triggers a workflow, it posts a confirmation in the thread with **Approve** and **Reject** buttons.

## Example prompts

Slack Agent works best when prompts include a clear identifier (user distinct ID, object ID, workflow slug, tenant key). Paste these in, swap your own values, and send.

**Users and preferences**

```text
@suprsend Show the profile and preferences for user distinct_id "user_123".

@suprsend Why isn't jane@acme.com getting emails for the payment-reminder workflow?

@suprsend Opt user_456 out of the product-updates category.
```

**Workflows and events**

```text
@suprsend List workflows in this workspace and summarize what each one does.

@suprsend Trigger the order-confirmation workflow for user jane@acme.com with a sample payload.

@suprsend Track a checkout-completed event for user_123 with order_id "ORD-9912".
```

**Analytics**

```text
@suprsend What were our email delivery and open rates last week?

@suprsend Break down WhatsApp delivery by tenant for the last 30 days.
```

**Documentation**

```text
@suprsend How do tenant default preferences interact with user-level overrides?

@suprsend What's the difference between objects and lists in SuprSend?
```

## Settings

Slack App setup lives on the same page as the In-product Agent: **Account Settings → [AI & Agents](https://app.suprsend.com/en/account-settings/ai-agents)**.

Connecting Slack is **admin-only**. Once an admin connects the workspace, anyone in Slack whose email matches a user in your SuprSend organization can DM `@SuprSend` or tag it in a channel.

From the settings page, an admin can:

- Connect a Slack workspace with **Connect to Slack**. The row then shows **Connected** and your Slack workspace ID.
- View Slack-attributed credit usage alongside the rest of your AI consumption.

To disconnect, uninstall the SuprSend app from your Slack workspace. SuprSend's notification infrastructure works the same without it.
